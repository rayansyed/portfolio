About

I'm a UX/UI designer based in Milton, Ontario, with an Honors Bachelor's in Computer Science from Sheridan College. I currently work as a Cloud Developer at BMO, building APIs on AWS — but my first real product design work happened back at Sheridan, prototyping and usability-testing a mobile app for a nonprofit client. Outside of work, I do photography, which is where a lot of my eye for composition and light actually comes from.

  • Real design process — usability testing, personas, and journey maps from client work, not just theory.
  • Full-stack fluency — years shipping production APIs on AWS, plus React and Swift.
  • A photographer's eye — composition, light, and framing from photography.
